# [App Name Placeholder] - Find Your Perfect Study Spot

## 🎯 The Problem
Finding a good place to study is a constant struggle for college students and remote workers. It's hard to know if a cafe has reliable Wi-Fi, enough outlets, or the right noise level before you get there. Furthermore, studying can be isolating, and coordinating with friends is disjointed.

## 💡 The Solution
[App Name] is a mobile-first web app that takes the guesswork out of studying. It combines cafe discovery, detailed study-ability ratings (Wi-Fi, outlets, noise), and a social layer to see where your friends are studying and what they're listening to.
