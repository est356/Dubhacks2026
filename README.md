# StudyHopper - Find Your Perfect Study Spot

## The Problem
Finding a good place to study is a constant struggle for college students and remote workers. It is hard to know if a cafe has reliable Wi-Fi, enough outlets, or the right noise level before you get there. Furthermore, studying can be isolating, and coordinating with friends is disjointed.

## The Solution
StudyHopper is a mobile-first web app that takes the guesswork out of studying. It combines cafe discovery, detailed study-ability ratings (Wi-Fi, outlets, noise), and an AI assistant that recommends the best spot based on your current needs.

## The Demo Plan
1. Open the app to the interactive Google Map showing nearby cafes.
2. Open the Hops AI chat and ask for a recommendation like "quiet place with lots of outlets, close to campus."
3. Show the Hops response with real cafe cards, photos, and addresses pulled from our Supabase database.
4. Apply filters for Wi-Fi, outlets, and noise to narrow down the map.
5. Log a study session and show the live leaderboard updating on the friends page.
