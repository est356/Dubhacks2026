# User Personas

## 1. The Stressed CS Major (Alex)
*   **Needs:** High-speed Wi-Fi, lots of outlets, quiet.
*   **Pain Point:** Spends 30 mins wandering campus looking for an open seat.
*   **How we help:** The AI Cat instantly filters for "quiet" and "outlets" and gives a map route.

## 2. The Remote Worker (Sam)
*   **Needs:** Good food, background chatter, meeting-friendly.
*   **Pain Point:** Doesn't know which cafes will kick them out after an hour.
*   **How we help:** The "Busyness" and "Price Range" ratings from Google Maps.

## 3. The Social Studier (Jamie)
*   **Needs:** To know where their friends are.
*   **Pain Point:** Checking 5 different group chats to see if anyone is at the library.
*   **How we help:** The Friend Activity feed shows they are at a cafe and what they are listening to.
